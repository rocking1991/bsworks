How a project runs

  1. Plan the catalogue

    We go through your products, collections and must-have features on a call or WhatsApp.

  2. Quote and advance

    You receive a quote for the store; work begins with a 50% advance payment.

  3. Build and review

    I design and build the store, then you review it on your own phone and desktop and ask for changes.

  4. Launch and handover

    After final payment the store goes live, and I walk you through adding products and editing banners.

Frequently asked questions

Who owns the Shopify store?

You do. The store runs on a Shopify plan in your name, and I work inside your account with staff or collaborator access.

Can you customize my existing Shopify theme instead of building a new store?

Yes. For section edits, layout changes and bug fixes on a store that is already live, see Shopify theme customization.

How long does a Shopify store take?

Most stores are completed within 4–5 days. Large catalogues or custom features can take up to 10 days.

Do you build Shopify stores for businesses in Punjab?

Yes. The Shopify stores in my portfolio are for brands in Canada and Australia, and I’m equally happy to build for businesses in Patiala, across Punjab and the rest of India.

Should I choose Shopify or WooCommerce?

Both work well; the right choice depends on how much control you want and who will run the store. I’ve written a plain-English Shopify vs WooCommerce comparison for Indian businesses.
