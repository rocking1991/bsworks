Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Most projects are completed within 4–5 days, depending on the requirements and complexity. Larger or more complex projects may take up to 10 days.

How is a project priced?

Pricing depends on the type of website, required features, design complexity, and overall scope. Contact me with your requirements, and I'll provide a quote tailored to your project.

What is required to get started?

To begin a project, I require a 50% advance payment of the agreed project cost. The remaining 50% is due upon completion, before final delivery.

How can we discuss the project?

All project discussions and communication are handled through phone calls or WhatsApp chat. This keeps communication simple, direct, and convenient.

What technologies and platforms do you work with?

I work with Shopify, WordPress, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and other relevant web technologies to build responsive, functional, and user-friendly websites.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes! I build responsive websites that adapt to different screen sizes, including smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers.

Can you update or modify an existing website?

Absolutely. I can help with website redesigns, bug fixes, layout improvements, new features, performance enhancements, and other custom changes to your existing website.

What happens after the website is completed?

Once the project is complete, I'll review the final work with you and make sure it meets the agreed requirements. I'll also help with the handover and provide guidance on managing your website when needed.

Where are you based, and do you work with clients outside Patiala?

I’m based in Patiala, Punjab, India. I work with businesses in Patiala and across Punjab and India, and remotely with clients in Canada and Australia. Projects are discussed by phone or WhatsApp, so your location is never a barrier. See my web design and development services for what I can build for you.
